# Hostile Workplace

A 3D office stealth-prank game that runs in the browser. It's Thursday, you've had enough: cause as much chaos as you can between 9:00 and 5:00 without your suspicion meter hitting 100%.

Open `index.html` in a browser (Chrome/Edge/Firefox) — no build step. Three.js is loaded from jsDelivr.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Walk (Shift to run) |
| Mouse / arrow keys | Look |
| Space | Jump |
| E (hold) | Prank, pick up, interact |
| C | Crouch — hide behind partitions and see sight cones on the floor |
| F | Fart (people nearby blame each other) |
| Click | Throw what you're holding, or shove chairs, knock things over and pop balloons |
| G | Drop what you're holding |
| Tab | Show/hide the to-do list |
| M | Mute |
| Esc | Pause |

## How it works

- **Suspicion:** anyone who sees you mid-prank, sneaking, sprinting or snooping in the boss's office gets suspicious. The boss counts extra. Pretend to work at your desk to cool off.
- **22 pranks:** post-it bomb, stapler in jello, whoopee cushion, KICK ME note, a reply-all love email from the boss, scrambled keyboard, sunken chair, a printer full of clowns, unplugging the server, fake free pizza, clogged toilet, rubber ducks, smashed monitors, a 35°C heat wave, a full blackout, indoor sprinklers, a meeting room full of balloons, an air horn on the boss's door, a glitter bomb, cat videos on the meeting TV, a beeping alarm clock in the ceiling and a toppled vending machine.
- **Smarter coworkers:** they stop and stare when something is off, hear you sprint or land a jump, search the last place they saw you, and run to tell the boss when they catch you mid-prank. The boss then searches the area.
- **Chaos:** chaos earned within 6 seconds stacks into a combo multiplier (up to ×3). Office chairs roll and bounce, props fly, balloons drift and pop.
- **Rendering:** image-based reflections, bloom, a color grade with vignette, heat tint and shake aberration. Set GRAPHICS to Low on the title screen for slower PCs.
- **Events:** meetings at 11:00 and 3:00 (skipping them is suspicious), and coworkers investigate loud noises.
