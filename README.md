# Hostile Workplace

A 3D office stealth-prank game that runs in the browser. It's Thursday, you've had enough: cause as much chaos as you can between 9:00 and 5:00 without your suspicion meter hitting 100%.

Open `index.html` in a browser (Chrome/Edge/Firefox) — no build step. Three.js is loaded from jsDelivr.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Walk (Shift to run) |
| Mouse / arrow keys | Look |
| E (hold) | Prank, pick up, interact |
| C | Crouch — hide behind partitions and see sight cones on the floor |
| F | Fart (people nearby blame each other) |
| Click / G | Throw / drop what you're holding |
| Tab | Show/hide the to-do list |
| M | Mute |
| Esc | Pause |

## How it works

- **Suspicion:** anyone who sees you mid-prank, sneaking, sprinting or snooping in the boss's office gets suspicious. The boss counts extra. Pretend to work at your desk to cool off.
- **13 pranks:** post-it bomb, stapler in jello, whoopee cushion, KICK ME note, a reply-all love email from the boss, scrambled keyboard, sunken chair, a printer full of clowns, unplugging the server, fake free pizza, clogged toilet, rubber ducks and smashed monitors.
- **Events:** meetings at 11:00 and 3:00 (skipping them is suspicious), and coworkers investigate loud noises.
